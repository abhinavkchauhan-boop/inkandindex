What This Is

SEO that builds over time instead of stopping when you do

One-off SEO work moves the needle once. A monthly retainer moves it every month — compounding. Each piece of content, each technical fix, each new citation makes the next month's results easier to achieve. That is the difference between a one-time improvement and a business that keeps climbing.

The retainer starts with a full audit in month one, then becomes consistent monthly execution — on-page work, content, GBP management, and reporting. You get one person handling everything, and a clear report every month showing exactly what was done and what it achieved.

Questions

FAQ

What is the minimum commitment?

There is no lock-in. It is a monthly rolling agreement with one month's notice to cancel. I would rather earn your business month to month than trap you in a contract.

When will I start seeing results?

GBP improvements are often visible within the first month. Organic ranking movement typically starts around month 2 to 3. Month 6 onwards is where compounding results become clearly measurable. SEO is not instant — but it lasts.

What do I need to provide to get started?

Access to your website, Google Search Console, Google Analytics, and your Google Business Profile. If you do not have some of these set up, I will set them up as part of month one.
