What This Is

The three Google listings that get all the bookings

When someone searches "homestay in Kasol" or "best cafe in Manali" on their phone, Google shows a map with three businesses before anything else. That is the Local Pack. Most clicks go to those three. Local SEO is the work of getting your business into that position — and keeping it there.

Himachal Pradesh is one of India's most searched travel destinations. The demand is already there. Tourists are searching for exactly what local businesses offer, right now, every day. The gap is that most businesses here are either absent from Google entirely or poorly set up. That gap is the opportunity.

Questions

FAQ

How long does Local SEO take to show results?

Most businesses see GBP impressions increase within the first month. Local Pack rankings typically start moving within 6 to 8 weeks. Significant, consistent results compound from month 3 onwards.

Do I need a website to benefit from Local SEO?

No. A well-optimised Google Business Profile can generate calls, direction requests, and bookings on its own. A website amplifies the results significantly, but it is not a requirement to start.

What is the difference between Local SEO and regular SEO?

Local SEO focuses on appearing in map-based searches and the Local Pack. Regular SEO targets organic rankings in the main search results. Both matter and they work together — Local SEO is typically the higher-priority starting point for physical businesses.

Can I cancel the monthly retainer if it is not working?

Yes. It is a monthly rolling agreement. I ask for one month's notice. I also report on results every month so you always know exactly what you are getting.
