What This Is

Why every SEO engagement starts with an audit

An SEO audit is a complete health check of your website — technical issues, on-page problems, content gaps, and off-page signals all reviewed together. Without one, any SEO work is guesswork. With one, you have a clear priority order: fix this first, then this, then this.

The audit becomes your roadmap. It tells you what is breaking your rankings, what is close to ranking with a small push, and what to ignore entirely. One clear document that turns a confusing problem into a concrete action plan.

Questions

FAQ

What is the difference between a surface audit and a full audit?

A surface audit is a 10-point check I do in about 30 minutes, usually as part of a free initial conversation. A full audit is comprehensive: 60 to 100 data points, delivered as a proper report with priorities and recommendations.

Do I need to be technical to understand the report?

No. Every finding is written in plain language. Technical terms are explained. The priority order tells you exactly where to start, even if you have never done SEO before.

Can you implement the fixes after the audit?

Yes. The audit is a natural starting point for an SEO retainer. If you want me to handle the implementation, we can move straight into a monthly package after the audit is delivered.
