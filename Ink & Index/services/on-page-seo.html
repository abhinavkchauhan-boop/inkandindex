What This Is

Making sure Google understands every page of your site

On-page SEO is the work done directly on your website pages — title tags, meta descriptions, heading structure, content, internal links, and image optimisation. These are the signals Google reads to understand what each page is about and whether it deserves to rank for a given search.

Most Himachal tourism sites have the right content but the wrong structure. A hotel page that says "welcome to our beautiful property" ranks for nothing. The same page, rewritten and structured around what people actually search, becomes a booking driver. That is the difference on-page SEO makes.

Questions

FAQ

Do you rewrite my content or just fix the technical tags?

Both, depending on what the page needs. Sometimes a title tag fix is enough. Sometimes the content itself needs to be restructured around the right keywords. I will tell you which is which in the audit.

How long does on-page SEO take to show results?

Google typically re-crawls and re-ranks updated pages within 2 to 6 weeks. For pages that were significantly under-optimised, ranking movement is often visible within the first month.

What do I need to provide to get started?

Access to your website's backend or CMS, and your Google Search Console if you have it set up. That is all. I handle the research and strategy from there.
