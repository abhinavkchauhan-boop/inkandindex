My Story

How I got here

I grew up in Dharamshala — not as a tourist, as a local. I left at sixteen to compete in boxing, became a five-time Himachal State Champion, won national bronze twice, and made it to the Under-21 Indian national team after Khelo India 2020. A knee injury ended the sport.

After that I found writing. I studied Tourism at Panjab University, taught myself SEO, and spent four years as a Senior Content Writer in Chandigarh. At some point I noticed that the hotels, cafes, and trek operators back home were invisible on Google. That became the problem I decided to solve.
