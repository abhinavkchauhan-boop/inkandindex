What This Is

Content built around what your customers actually search for

Most tourism businesses in Himachal publish content randomly — a blog post here, a page update there — with no connection to how customers actually search. Content strategy changes that. It starts with keyword research, identifies the topics that bring the right visitors, and builds a plan for creating content that ranks and converts.

By month three you have a content structure working for you. By month six, individual pages are ranking for searches your competitors are not targeting. The strategy is the difference between publishing into the void and building an asset that brings in bookings month after month.

Questions

FAQ

Do you write the content or just plan it?

Both. The strategy includes the plan — keyword research, calendar, and briefs. The writing is included in the monthly work. You get content that is planned to rank and written to be read.

How long before content starts ranking?

New content typically gets indexed within 1 to 2 weeks. Initial rankings appear within 4 to 8 weeks. Consistent publishing compounds — by month 4 to 6 you will have multiple pages generating organic traffic every month.

What do I need to provide to get started?

Access to your website and Google Search Console if you have it. Basic information about your business, services, and the type of customers you want to reach. I handle the research and writing from there.
